🌟 Our Origin Story

Why I built this game: Like many parents, evening spelling practice used to be a daily battle filled with tears, frustration, and rote memorization drills that simply didn't stick .

I realized we needed a system that removed the pain while building true mastery . I designed this game around a pain-free mastery loop: instead of punishing mistakes, the app catches missed words, presents visual decoy spellings to train eye recognition, and loops them back gently until every single word is spelled with 100% confidence .

To bridge the gap between hard work and real-world rewards, I created the in-game TV-Time Piggy Bank . By converting active effort and list mastery into tangible screen time, daily spelling transformed overnight into a self-motivated, stress-free success !
