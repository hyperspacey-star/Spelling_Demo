📖 How It Works & Parent Guide

🏆 Defining Mastery

Mastery means all the words on the list are spelled absolutely perfectly without a single error on the first try. If a child uses a text hint or makes even one mistake on a word, they must practice until they can complete the list flawlessly.

🧠 The Pain-Free Mastery Loop

  • Permanent Visual Clues: Syllable counts and Word Types are permanently displayed on screen from the start, providing immediate visual reinforcement without giving away the word!
  • The 4-Heart Hint Lock: Clever mnemonics and target word spellings are strictly locked. A child must struggle independently and lose 4 hearts before the text hint unlocks.
  • Visual Decoy Training: Missed words trigger a multiple-choice review against dynamically generated decoy spellings, training visual word recognition before re-spelling.

💰 Piggy Bank Economy

Active practice earns background time that converts into tangible household rewards. To earn the $1.00 Jackpot and ⭐ Mastered Badge, a child must achieve full Mastery!
